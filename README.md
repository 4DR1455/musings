# What?
As you can read in the title, this is a place where I'd be posting my musings, the strange theories I think about and I'd like to share. Those can go from really technical things to very philosophical where no science or maths are involved. This is literally a place to avoid ideas and thoughts dying inside my mind before helping our society.
# Why?
As I am not famous... No one would come to take these ideas so I can store them here, and... I can show you my way of thinking, my exotic ideas, the challenges I like to take, and other things that I still don't know I'd like to share.
# How?
You will see documents. I'm not hiding anything; you will probably see from documents only containing a title to documents larger than a newspaper article. Every document is an idea, a thought I've got on a bus, on a walk, or wherever. Also notice I won't include every idea; ideas I'd like to try professionally may not be published due to obvious reasons.
Also know you will see writings in the first person like this one. Yep, I'm talking to myself, and I'm letting you peek into my thoughts. This is not formal and is not professional; this is a casual way I can show who I am.

**NO** document will ever be **finished**; they will just be more or less advanced. Don't expect everything to be correct. I am not aiming for that; I will miss things. That's why a writing is never finished: I write, miss something, notice it, correct it, write again, and so on. I may also be developing already developed theories, but I don't really care... I'm just exploring possibilities. If I'm really interested in a field, I will probably end up googling it and adding a description of what I've found. After that, I will probably question things and propose my own point of view. So, the most interesting parts (to me) won't be already developed.
